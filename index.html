Building Regulations Atlas

Map About Data Sources

The Building Regulations Atlas collates data from several World Bank Group resources on topics related to planning, design, and building control regulations and aspects of the wider building regulatory environment in selected cities and countries.

Data Availability

Full Data (60+ indicators)
B-READY Data (40-59)
Limited Data (<40)

Regulatory Topics Covered

The Atlas covers four main regulatory topics with detailed sub-topics for building design and compliance mechanisms.

📍

Planning and Zoning

Land use maps, hazard zones, trunk infrastructure requirements

🏗️

Building Design

Structural resilience, fire safety, green buildings, universal accessibility, services

🧱

Materials

Regulated materials, testing requirements, disposal procedures

✓

Compliance Mechanisms

Building control, transparency, dispute resolution, professional registration

Data Sources

Business Ready (B-READY)

2025

Assesses the regulatory framework and public services directed at firms, and the efficiency with which regulatory framework and public services are combined in practice.

Global Assessment of Building Codes

2025

Assesses the technical content of building codes for selected countries and related code implementation mechanisms.

Building Green Global Dataset

2025

Contains data on building energy codes and standards applicable to new and existing buildings, as well as their enforcement mechanisms and levels of compliance.

Building Regulations in Sub-Saharan Africa

2023

Contains data on building regulatory frameworks in Sub-Saharan African countries with a focus on building design regulations and their implementation.
