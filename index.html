About the Atlas

The Building Regulations Atlas collates data from several World Bank and IFC resources on topics related to planning, design, and building control regulations and aspects of the wider building regulatory environment in selected cities and countries. The Atlas covers four main regulatory topics and a number of sub-topics for building design regulations and compliance mechanisms. Users can select data by country or city, and/or by selected themes and indicator types. In addition to visualizing selected data on a map, detailed data can be exported as a .csv file or pdf.

Regulatory Topics Covered

📍

Planning and Zoning

Land use maps, hazard zones, infrastructure requirements

🏗️

Building Design

General provisions, structural resilience, fire safety, green buildings, accessibility, services

🧱

Materials

Regulated materials, testing requirements, disposal

✓

Compliance Mechanisms

Building control, transparency, dispute resolution, professional registration

2025

Assesses the technical content of building codes for selected countries and related code implementation mechanisms.

Building Green Global Dataset

2025

Contains data on building energy codes and standards applicable to new and existing buildings, as well as their enforcement mechanisms and levels of compliance.

Building Regulations in Sub-Saharan Africa

2023

Contains data on building regulatory frameworks in Sub-Saharan African countries with a focus on building design regulations and their implementation.
